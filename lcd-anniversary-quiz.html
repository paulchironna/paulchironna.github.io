LCD Anniversary Quiz Game

Schematic. Arduino-based quiz game with a 20×4 I²C LCD, four answer buttons, and a 24-LED heart driven by three daisy-chained 74HC595 shift registers.

Shift-register prototype. Early test of the 74HC595 chain verifying clock/latch/data timing and LED mapping before final assembly.

Final assembly. Board-level wiring, button placement, and LCD mounting on perfboard prior to enclosure.

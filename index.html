Rigol DS1054Z — Four-Channel Input Failure

Signal injection at the BNC center pin isolated open series resistors in the input path on all four channels. Repaired with 82Ω 1% SMD rework and verified against a 1 kHz square wave.

Motor Control · Field Service

Husqvarna PG280 — Motor Overload Fault

A ~45 A current reading downstream of the overload isolated a tripping 110V CSCR motor to the motor side rather than the controls. Teardown found thermal damage at a start capacitor terminal; replaced the capacitors and cutoff module, re-terminated the harness, and confirmed the cutoff engaged at speed.
